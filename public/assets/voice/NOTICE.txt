Junior Space Commander — prepared narration credits

SI-generated audio: Rocky and Mission Control narration was generated offline
with Tiny Voca and Dizione. Each MP3 includes this disclosure in its ID3 comment;
manifest.json records SI generation and the tools used. No human voice is cloned.

The game plays authored, pre-recorded MP3 narration only. It contains no voice
cloning, voice synthesis, downloadable inference model, browser worker, or
personalized-voice feature. It never records a microphone.

The prepared recordings were created offline with Tiny Voca and Dizione by
Muhammed Nazeem (n4ze3m):
https://github.com/n4ze3m/tiny-voca
https://github.com/n4ze3m/dizione

All dialogue is authored game content. No dataset or training is included.
